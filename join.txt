An INNER JOIN filters out any users who haven't placed any completed orders—they vanish from the report entirely because they don't have a matching row in the orders table.

LEFT JOIN (The "Keep Everything on the Left" Rule)
A LEFT JOIN keeps all rows from the left table (the one written before the JOIN keyword), whether they have a match in the right table or not.

RIGHT JOIN
A RIGHT JOIN does the exact opposite of a LEFT JOIN. It keeps all rows from the right table (the one written after the JOIN keyword), and pulls in matching data from the left table. If a row on the right doesn't match the left, the left side's columns get filled with NULL.


INNER JOIN Only matching rows on both sides,The row is deleted from the results.
LEFT JOIN All rows from the left table + matches on the right,"Keeps the left row, puts NULL for the missing right data."
RIGHT JOIN All rows from the right table + matches on the left,"Keeps the right row, puts NULL for the missing left data."
